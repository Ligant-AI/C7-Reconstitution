The vial

Required. Not inferred, defaulted or suggested, including for named reagents. Activity content (IU, U) accepts scientific notation, e.g. 1e6.

What the stated content is the content of

Content basis: the reagent (activity). Activity is of the active reagent and of nothing else, so no other basis is offered.

A portion taken from a bulk solid is weighed and entered as the content itself: "weighed, from bulk".
